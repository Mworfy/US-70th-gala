Through the decades

Photos and milestones from seventy years of AIESEC in the United States.

1948. After World War II, seven young people across seven countries start AIESEC with one dream: build understanding between nations, one person and one internship at a time.

  1. 1950s

    1956. AIESEC in the United States is founded at Columbia University, bringing a global youth leadership movement to American campuses.

  2. 1960s

  3. 1970s

    1977. AIESEC reaches 50 countries on six continents, with more than 40,000 young people having taken part. The United States plays a vital role in that growth.

  4. 1980s

  5. 1990s

  6. 2000s

  7. 2010s

    2010. AIESEC delivers more than 10,000 exchange experiences in a single year for the first time, a milestone the United States helped drive.

    2015. Youth leaders from 126 countries and territories gather at United Nations Headquarters to champion the Sustainable Development Goals.

  8. 2020s

    2026. Seventy years after Columbia, alumni, members, and partners gather in Chicago to celebrate and to look ahead.
